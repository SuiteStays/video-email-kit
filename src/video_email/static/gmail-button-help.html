__COMPANY__ Video

Install the Chrome button (one time, about 2 minutes)

Works in Chrome and Microsoft Edge on a computer. It adds a __COMPANY__ Video button to the toolbar and a Video button inside Gmail.

  1. Download the Chrome button. It saves video-button.zip to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open your Downloads folder, right-click the file, choose Extract All, then Extract. Keep the folder it makes (don't delete it later).
  3. In Chrome, click the address bar, type chrome://extensions and press Enter. (In Edge type edge://extensions.)
  4. Turn on Developer mode (switch in the top-right corner; in Edge it's on the left).
  5. Click Load unpacked and choose the gmail-button folder inside the folder you extracted.
  6. Click the puzzle-piece icon to the right of the address bar and click the pin next to __COMPANY__ Video, so the button always shows.

Record from anywhere: click the blue __COMPANY__ Video button in the toolbar (or press Alt+Shift+V) and pick Camera, Screen, or Screen + camera.

Screenshots: the same button takes the whole page (Alt+Shift+P), the visible part, or Select an area (any monitor) (Alt+Shift+A): pick the monitor or window in Chrome's list, then drag a box on the frozen screen - like the Snipping Tool. For menus that close when you click, use Select an area in 3 seconds. The result opens straight away so you can mark it up, copy, download or send it.

Video button on other websites (off unless you want it): on a site where you message customers (for example your customer inbox), click the __COMPANY__ Video button and tick Video button in message boxes on this site. A small Video button then appears on that site's message boxes only - never on sign-in pages, search boxes or other sites.

Inside Gmail: put the person in the To box, click where you want the video, then click the blue Video button next to Send.

Already installed the older Gmail-only button? Extract the new download over the old folder, then on chrome://extensions click the round reload arrow on __COMPANY__ Video.
